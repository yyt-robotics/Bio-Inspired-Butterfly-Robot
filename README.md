# Bio-Inspired-Butterfly-Robot

A lightweight bio-inspired flapping-wing robot project developed for the National College Students Mechanical Innovation Design Competition in China.

## Overview

This project focused on the design and development of a butterfly-inspired flapping-wing robotic system. The goal was to create a lightweight mechanism capable of generating controllable wing-flapping motion while maintaining a compact structure.

The project involved mechanical design, motion mechanism development, lightweight material selection, prototype assembly, aerodynamic consideration, and iterative testing.

## Key Features

- Bio-inspired flapping-wing mechanism
- Lightweight mechanical structure
- Carbon fiber and PET-based wing design
- Custom actuation and transmission mechanism
- CAD-based mechanical design
- Prototype assembly and testing
- Competition-oriented engineering presentation
